BURAT WARD M.C.A.
BURAT WARD Civic Engagement & Ward Development Platform 

BURAT WARD is a civic technology project designed to improve transparency, public participation, and accountability in ward-level development.

The Problem 

Residents often lack a central, accessible way to follow local development projects, raise concerns, and engage with their representatives.

Our Goal 

Build a digital platform connecting residents, ward leadership, and community development activities.

Planned Features Ward announcements and public information Development project tracking Community feedback and service requests Public participation Administrative dashboard Development progress reporting Technology 

The platform's implementation and technology stack are documented as development progresses.

Project Status 

Early-stage development. Features will be documented as they are implemented and tested.
